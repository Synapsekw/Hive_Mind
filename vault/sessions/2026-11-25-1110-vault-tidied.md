# The Vault, tidied

## What changed

- Round 2 of the interface rework. Danijel asked to see the Vault before any change, so the screen was first photographed in nine states and twelve problems listed; he picked layout "A · Three columns, tidied" from three clickable mocks. Spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-25-vault-tidy-design.md`, plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-25-vault-tidy.md`. Merged as `161de56`.
- **Top bar:** tabs, the filter field, and one **Add notes** menu (Import a folder, Connect wiki links, Attach topics, each with a line saying what it does). The three panels open as a dialog over the Vault card. Shared with me has no Add notes.
- **Rows** show up to two topics and "+N"; sharing is an icon whose words are its tooltip and screen-reader text. The folder control reads "Folder sharing: Everyone" or "Folder sharing: none". Empty folders without a rule are hidden; "Held" reads "Held, waiting for you".
- **Note pane:** title once (a first heading equal to the title is not shown), one meta line, one action row (sharing, topic chips with ×, **+ Topic** picker with search, **⋯** with Copy link and Delete note…). With no note open, a summary: counts, held notes with Review, the six last changed.
- **Narrow:** under a 1,000 px card the folder column folds into a "Choose folder or topic" button on the list title.
- Gates on the merged state: typecheck, lint, 320 + 2,166 unit tests, build; 204 vault, fold and rail browser tests; the full suite (423) passed on the branch before the last rebase.

## Why

The Vault put three rare import actions where the filter belongs, repeated the sharing words on every row, left a large empty pane, showed the title twice and stacked management controls above the text. The aim was fewer, quieter controls and more room to read, now that the real vault (1,641 notes, 287 topics) is in it.

## How to test (for the user)

1. Pull `develop`, restart `pnpm dev:web`, sign in and open `/vault` (or `/dev/vault` for sample data). The bar reads: tabs, a wide filter, **Add notes ▾**.
2. With no note open, the right side shows Main's counts (notes, folders, topics, changed today), the held notes with **Review**, and the six last changed notes. Press **Review**: the list shows only held notes.
3. Rows show topic chips (Zain, Aldar, …) and a small ● or ◐ at the right; point at it for who can read the note. No "Everyone, from …" words on the rows.
4. Open a note: the title appears once; under it one grey line (path · changed · written by); then one row with sharing, topic chips, **+ Topic** and **⋯**.
5. Press **+ Topic**, type part of a topic's name, choose it: a chip appears. Press its ×: it goes.
6. Press **⋯** → **Copy link**: the status line says "Link copied." Press **⋯** → **Delete note…**: the usual question appears; press **Keep**.
7. Press **Add notes** → **Import a folder**: a dialog opens over the Vault; Escape closes it and focus is back on Add notes. Open **Shared with me**: there is no Add notes.
8. Choose a folder in the left column: its header says "Folder sharing: …". A folder with no notes and no rule is not listed.
9. Narrow the window to about 1,100 px: the folder column is gone; the list title is a button ("Main ▾" style). Press it, choose a folder: the list filters and the picker closes.
